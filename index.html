The problem

Two problems.
One material.

Plastic waste is filling landfills, while construction consumes huge amounts of raw materials and energy. RePlast connects the two challenges.

♻

Waste overload

▦

Resource heavy

How it works

From discarded
to designed.

A simple material journey — collecting waste, preparing it, forming it, and giving it a new purpose.

01

Collect

Plastic waste is identified and collected before it becomes landfill waste.

02

Prepare

The material is sorted and prepared for the RePlast production process.

03

Form

Prepared plastic is transformed into a block or brick format for potential construction use.

04

Build

The result is a new material concept designed to help reduce waste and raw-material dependence.
